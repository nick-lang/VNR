## Findings (what the literature says about our results)

1. **H9/H10 were predictable kills — and that *raises* the memory thesis's survival odds.** Git Re-Basin (2022): independently trained nets sit in permutation-distinct loss basins; naive averaging or whole-donor transplantation is expected to fail. The 2025 systematic LLM-merging study: of six merging methods, only Task Arithmetic works, and it *requires a shared base initialization*. Our donors were independently trained from random init — the exact regime where all merging fails. We rediscovered a known negative; the kills say little about memory per se and a lot about missing basin alignment. H14 (joint training = one shared basin) is precisely the escape route this literature prescribes, upgrading its rationale from plausible to literature-backed.
2. **The library thesis has real-ARC external validation we lacked.** Pang (ARC Prize 2025 runner-up): a DreamCoder-style growing cross-task library on real ARC reached 77.1% ARC-1 at ~$4/task, cutting LLM calls 36 -> 10 — measured amortization, the thing H5 showed synthetically. Caveat: it rides on a frontier LLM's priors. Counterweight: the EACL 2026 library-learning critique shows in-context LLM "library" gains often vanish at matched compute and reuse is rarely audited — so any future VNR library claim must be compute-matched with measured reuse (our Stage-1 design already does this; keep it).
3. **Concept-level beats instance-level memory — independently confirmed.** ArcMemo: decomposed, parameterized, typed concepts in memory beat whole-solution memories on ARC-AGI-1 (+7.5% relative, robust across inference scales). This is our combined H9+H10 inference ("a whole trained model is the wrong unit of memory; store parts") arrived at independently in the LLM substrate. Stage 5e's direction is corroborated; its storage format (parameterized abstractions with typed interfaces) is a direct design template.
4. **H14 expectation calibration (recorded BEFORE the run).** Fair meta-learning comparisons show plain multi-task initialization helps least exactly when task diversity is high — and ARC is maximally task-diverse by design. H14 as pre-registered is multi-task (round-robin), not bi-level meta-learning. Consequence, pre-registered now: **a marginal or kill H14 does not close the shared-backbone route; the escalation is a Reptile-style outer update in `s5f_runner.py`** (cheap: same jobs, different update rule). Also expect any wins to be family-local (H9's wins clustered by family).
5. **The clarified goal matches the field's own bottleneck statement.** ARC Prize Foundation 2026: the accuracy gap is "bottlenecked by engineering"; the **efficiency gap is bottlenecked by fundamental science and new ideas.** Living Survey: accuracy ~ 0.15 x log(cost) — 10x compute per 15 points. And the economics literature shows frontier-*level* usage cost is *rising* ~3-18x/yr (longer reasoning traces eat the per-token deflation), while distillation only compresses capability that already exists (needs a frontier teacher). So "cheap frontier capability" will not arrive by default, and the program's cost-first orientation targets the acknowledged open-science gap.
6. **Existence proof for the goal, narrow-domain version.** PTRM (TRM + noise-perturbed parallel rollouts + Q-head selection): ~2x frontier-LLM accuracy on a puzzle domain at <0.0001x the cost, 7M params, no retraining. Domain-limited, but it is what "frontier capability at near-zero cost" looks like when it exists: tiny recursive refinement + a learned verifier — the exact propose/verify slot of the VNR architecture.

## Decision

- **Plausibility verdict:** the VNR thesis is *unproven but strengthened* by this pass. Its two riskiest beliefs (cross-task library amortization on real tasks; parts-not-blobs memory) both acquired independent external evidence this cycle (Pang; ArcMemo). The kills we logged align with known negatives rather than contradicting the thesis.
- **"From the floor" verdict:** both extremes are already tested — hand-built floor (Stages 1-3c, died on coverage) and post-hoc grafting on CompressARC (H9/H10, died on basin misalignment). The literature points at the middle VNR already converged on: shared structure *created during training* (H14), then per-task deltas as the reusable unit (task-vector formulation), then decomposition into parameterized concepts (5e).
- **H14 design changes from this pass (pre-run):** (a) keep the pre-registered protocol unchanged for comparability; (b) pre-register the escalation path — if H14 is marginal/kill, run H14b with a Reptile-style outer step before abandoning the backbone route; (c) new cheap secondary readout: after joint training, save per-task deltas vs the backbone and report their pairwise cosine similarity (tests the task-vector geometry the merging literature predicts).
- **Goal framing (honest):** "as powerful as frontier models at near-zero cost" across all domains has no known mechanism; the defensible near-term form is frontier-*matching* capability per domain at orders-of-magnitude lower cost (PTRM-style), widened over time by a memory/adaptation architecture. Recorded in `docs/plan.md`.
