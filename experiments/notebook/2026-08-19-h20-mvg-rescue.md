# 2026-08-19 — H20: MVG rescue attempt (capacity + retention + hard transfer)

- **PoC:** `experiments/poc-vnr-s9-mvg/h20_runner.py` (+ `h20_result.json`)
- **Hypothesis:** H20 (pre-registered before the run; design amended pre-run after two assay-sensitivity probes, both recorded in the ledger)
- **Hardware:** CPU only, ~2 min, $0

## Setup

Owner asked whether MVG is completely dead after H19 or fixable. H20 removed
H19's three named confounds at once: binding capacity (top-4 score contest per
epoch), horizon-matched retention (EMA decay 0.8/epoch instead of binary
2-epoch forgetting), and a transfer set that can actually discriminate library
quality. Arms as in H19 (M ring / R exact permutation / F fixed; 5 seeds).

**Assay-sensitivity probes (run BEFORE the decision run; the method matters):**
budget-hardness turned out impossible — raw search solved 20/20 k=3 and 16/16
k=4 "hard" tasks because the DSL's group structure collapses deep compositions
into short equivalents (>half of 6-primitive programs have <=3-primitive
equivalents, measured); and even a PERFECT 6-concept oracle library beats raw
by only ~10-13pp at the best node budget — BELOW the pre-registered accept
bar, i.e., the assay as first designed could not detect the effect it was
registered to detect. Fix (pre-run amendment): depth-cap transfer at
max_depth=3, where the oracle probe gives raw 57% vs oracle 100% — a 43pp
ceiling. Measuring the assay ceiling against the pre-registered effect size
before running is now standing practice.

## Result: INCONCLUSIVE per the rule — artifact confirmed, effect negligible

- Pooled transfer solve rate: **M 0.933 vs R 0.913** (+2.0pp; accept needed
  +10pp), with **M >= R in 5/5 seeds**. Raw baseline 0.540 — the library is
  worth +39pp; the curriculum is worth ~2pp.
- **H19's kill is now explained:** with a sane retention policy the modular
  order is never worse and directionally better in every seed — the M-worse
  result was the forgetting-horizon artifact, exactly as diagnosed.
- **But the artifact-free MVG effect is ~2pp under a 43pp ceiling.** Mechanism:
  BPE proposal statistics are order-invariant, so both orders converge to
  near-identical libraries (alignment readouts match); temporal structure can
  only act through retention dynamics, and fixing those equalizes the arms.

## Decision

- Per H20's pre-registered closure commitment (no third symbolic variant):
  **the MVG question is closed for the symbolic/batch-compression substrate.**
  One artifact-contaminated kill (H19) + one artifact-free null-to-tiny result
  (H20) under an adequate assay ceiling.
- MVG survives solely as a candidate pre-registered arm of H18 in an
  order-sensitive (SGD) learner — where the Kashtan-Alon mechanism (structure
  shaped by the ORDER of selection pressure) actually exists — with a lowered
  prior and two lessons attached: retention horizon must exceed goal-return
  period, and the assay ceiling must be measured before the run.
- Positive by-product worth keeping: the utility-contest library (capacity +
  EMA retention) is a working curation mechanism (+39pp over raw at depth-
  capped transfer, no bloat) — a validated ingredient for H18's curation
  design, independent of MVG.
