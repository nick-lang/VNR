# 2026-08-19 — H19: MVG-curriculum pilot on the symbolic substrate

- **PoC:** `experiments/poc-vnr-s9-mvg/h19_runner.py` (+ `h19_result.json`)
- **Hypothesis:** H19 (pre-registered 2026-08-19 in `ledger/hypotheses.md` before the full run)
- **Hardware:** CPU only (ran after the E1-B GPU drain), ~3 min, $0

## Setup

Kashtan & Alon's modularly-varying-goals claim, transplanted to the Stage-1
library harness. Arm M: 12 epochs of concept-pair goals on a shared-subgoal
ring. Arm R: the SAME 120 tasks shuffled (exact permutation control). Arm F:
fixed pair. Persistent cap-12 library; per-epoch BPE proposals; Minton-style
pruning after 2 zero-use epochs. Primary: median nodes-to-solution on 30
never-seen concept combinations with each arm's final library; 5 seeds.

## Result: KILL (unanimous)

- M/R per-seed ratios: 1.14, 9.06, 2.04, 2.09, 1.14 — M WORSE in 5/5 seeds
  (pooled 1.25; accept needed <= 0.75 in >= 4/5). Solve rate 150/150 in every
  arm (the task space binds on search cost, not solvability, as designed).
- Sanity: libraries beat raw primitives ~2.6x (median transfer nodes 113 vs
  294) — H5's amortization replicates; the KILL is about temporal structure,
  not about libraries.
- Concept alignment low everywhere (M keeps ~1 aligned macro, R ~0): BPE learns
  search shortcuts, not the generative concepts (pre-registered caveat 2).

## The mechanism (why the shuffled arm won)

The pre-registered threat fired in an unexpected direction. It was not BPE
order-invariance that neutralized the curriculum — it was the RETENTION POLICY
that punished it: under modular switching, 2-epoch utility-forgetting prunes
macros whose concepts return 5 epochs later; under random order, every useful
macro gets occasional use and survives. The two dossier imports interact
destructively whenever the forgetting horizon is shorter than the goal-return
period. This is itself a Minton-literature lesson arriving the hard way:
utility must be measured on the workload, and a modular workload has LONG
return periods.

## Decision

- H19 closed per the pre-registered rule. No variant runs without a new
  pre-registration (candidate: retention horizon >= goal-return period, or
  utility-weighted rather than binary forgetting).
- H18 consequence: an MVG arm in the neural substrate remains theoretically
  motivated (SGD is order-sensitive in ways corpus BPE is not) but has LOST its
  cheap-validation support; if run, its pre-registration must fix the
  retention-horizon coupling this pilot exposed.
- Write-up: no MVG claim enters the current paper (Stage-9 scope). The
  policy-curriculum coupling finding is recorded here and in the hypotheses
  ledger for the H18 design.
